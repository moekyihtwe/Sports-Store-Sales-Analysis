# Sports-Store-Sales-Analysis
![Sales Overview](images/Overview)
1️⃣ Category Performance

Bikes generated the highest revenue (~$11M)

However, margin contribution appears weaker compared to revenue size

Accessories and Clothing show stronger relative margin efficiency

📌 Insight: High-revenue categories are not necessarily high-profit categories.
Cost structure optimization is crucial for Bikes.

2️⃣ Sales vs COGS Analysis

Total COGS is close to total sales, resulting in a 10% overall margin.

📌 Insight:
Small increases in COGS significantly impact profitability.
Improving supplier pricing or adjusting pricing strategy could materially improve profit.

3️⃣ Market Performance

United States is the dominant revenue contributor (~$10.4M)

UK, Germany, and France contribute significantly less

📌 Insight:
The business is heavily dependent on the US market.
Geographic diversification could reduce risk exposure.

4️⃣ Demographic Insights

The 35–55 age group (Core Earners) contributes the highest sales (~$10.9M)

25–34 Adults follow as second strongest segment

17–24 and 55+ contribute significantly less
